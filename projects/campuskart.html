ERP Consulting & Implementation

CampusKart — Omni-Channel ERP Transformation

An end-to-end ERP consulting and implementation project: business-process diagnosis, BPMN engineering, Odoo solution design, configuration and transactional validation.

BUSINESS CHALLENGE

From fragmented processes to an integrated operating model

CampusKart's project context included demand spikes, fragmented cross-channel inventory and manual B2B budget clearance. The transformation objective was to create a unified cloud ERP connecting Sales, Procurement, Inventory and Finance.

PROCESS ENGINEERING

O2C · P2P · M2O

Re-engineered three core workflows using BPMN 2.0 in Bizagi, mapping roles, decisions and cross-functional handoffs across Sales, Warehouse, Procurement and Finance.
